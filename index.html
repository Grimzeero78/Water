HOW TO USE TAPTAMER

  1. PRESS the top knob down - a quick rinse under 10 seconds that closes itself (a dashpot bleeds it shut). Press again any time to stop.
  2. PULL the knob up - a 3-minute wash. A hidden escapement counts it down and shuts off at zero. No dial, no display, no electronics.
  3. TURN the knob (drag it sideways, or use the flow slider) - opens like a normal tap: the more you turn, the more water passes. Turn back to close.
