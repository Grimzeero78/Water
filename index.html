HOW TO USE TAPTAMER

  1. Pick a time - press 3 MIN or 7 MIN at the bottom, or drag the brass dial (quick-click the dial for a 3-min run).
  2. Water flows while the clockwork counts down and the dial unwinds to zero.
  3. It shuts itself off at zero - or twist back to zero / press STOP to close early.

Every opening is a timed countdown - max 10 minutes, then it shuts itself off. There is no stay-on position. A tap left running for an hour wastes about 480 L; TapTamer caps every use at the time you twist.

INSIDE THE HEAD (GHOST VIEW)

Mainspring - wound by your twist
Gear train - slows the release
Escapement - ticks off the seconds
Cam drum + push-rod - rides down as it unwinds
Latch - holds the valve open, trips at zero
Poppet valve + closing spring
Water path - tap to outlet
