HOW TO USE TAPTAMER

  1. TURN the knob sideways to set a flow limit - how much water may pass. Turning alone never runs water: a second valve behind it stays shut. That is the safety.
  2. PRESS the knob down for a quick rinse that closes itself in about 8 seconds - or simply hold it down while you need water.
  3. PULL the knob up for a 3-minute wash. An oil dashpot bleeds it shut at zero. No clockwork, no dial, no electronics.
Turn fully back to zero and the tap is locked - even pressing gives nothing. The knob is only the handle: everything lives in one head cartridge on top - the column below is a plain pipe with nothing inside it. TapTamer is a whole replacement tap: fitting is one swap, servicing is one lid. Keys: 1 press · 2 pull · ←/→ turn · L lock · 0 stop · E explode the model to see every part

Two valves in series packed into one compact head on top: your turn only sets a limit, the spring poppet behind it decides if any water passes - so a turn can never be left open. Press = a self-closing 8 s rinse, pull = a 3-minute wash, both shut themselves off. Turn to zero and even pressing gives nothing. Nothing hangs on a pipe and nothing sits down the column - the whole mechanism is one cartridge you drop into the head, so fitting is one tap-swap and servicing is one lid (same basin hole, same 1/2" tail, same hose). A normal tap left running an hour wastes about 480 L - TapTamer's worst case is one 3-minute wash, about 24 L.
